# 📊 Power BI Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, quantity, customer segments, product categories, regions, states, and sub-categories.

The dashboard provides a multi-level view of business performance, allowing users to move from high-level sales KPIs to detailed **regional, category, sub-category, and segment-level analysis**.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **data analysis, data visualization, dashboard design, DAX, data modeling, and business intelligence**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the highest-performing countries and states.
* Compare sales and profit across different years and quarters.
* Analyze customer segments and their contribution to sales.
* Understand profitability across product categories.
* Analyze sales and profit at the sub-category level.
* Compare regional and segment-level performance.
* Build an interactive dashboard with filters and navigation.
* Present business insights through clear and interactive visualizations.

## 📂 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of **three analytical pages**.

### 1️⃣ Sales Dashboard

The main dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance.

#### Key KPIs

* **Total Revenue:** 2M
* **Total Profit:** 289K
* **Total Quantity:** 6K

#### Visualizations

* Sales and Profit by Year
* Sales by Year and Quarter
* Top 10 Countries by Sales
* Sales and Profit by Customer Segment
* Profit by Category
* Order Date filter
* KPI cards for Revenue, Profit, and Quantity

#### Key Observations

* The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of sales.
* **Technology** generates the highest profit among the major categories.
* Sales show an overall increasing trend toward the later years.
* Australia, China, and India are among the leading countries by sales.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 💰 Sales & Profit

The dashboard indicates a positive overall sales trend, with sales increasing toward the later years of the analyzed period.

### 👥 Customer Segments

The **Consumer segment** is the largest contributor to sales, followed by Corporate and Home Office.

### 🛍️ Product Categories

Technology represents the strongest category in terms of profit contribution, while Furniture and Office Supplies provide additional contributions.

### 🌎 Geographic Performance

The dashboard highlights significant differences in sales performance across countries, states, and cities, allowing businesses to identify high-performing markets.

### 📦 Sub-Category Performance

Sub-category analysis helps identify products with strong sales and profitability as well as areas that may require further investigation.

### 📅 Time-Based Analysis

Yearly and quarterly visualizations provide insights into seasonal patterns and changes in sales and profit over time.
